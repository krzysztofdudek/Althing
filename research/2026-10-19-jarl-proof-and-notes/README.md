# Jarl: Proof, Notes and the merge record

**Question:** One Evidence section of a Jarl issue holds seven kinds of entry: the worker's rows, the merger's check row, free text, two kinds of ruling, the reasons of a drop or a deferral, and retractions. The merger's row counts for the done gate exactly as the worker's does. The design note issue 333 of the 6.2.0 loop asks for before any code has to settle five things: how the section splits into Proof and Notes with the merge check in the merge record, how existing loops are read, whether the split must raise the record format, what the journal, the gate, the views, `record.mjs` and Horde see, and whether a smaller design meets the acceptance. It also has to say what follows for issues 321 and 251.

**Method:** Read JarlSkill's `jarl-lib.mjs`, `record.mjs`, the record contract test, the format marker test and the frozen earlier reader at the tip of `release/6.2.0` (`3f6fbf8`), and Horde's evidence writes and body edits (read-only). Listed the writer, the readers and the gate's use of each kind of entry. Counted the kinds of entry in a frozen copy of the release loop (337 issues, 2,801 log lines). Probed scratch loops with the tip (the merger's row closing an issue alone) and with the frozen earlier reader's command line. The frozen reader ran over the split form as specified, with sub-headings, and over the alternative with two top-level headings: done with and without a worker row, the report's reasons, show, and a write.

**Status:** concluded

**Conclusion:** The fix is that `merged` stops writing its check as a row: the check goes to the merge record (a `Merge check` header field beside Merged and CI, and the merge line's `check` as today), so only `evidence` writes proof, and the gate's code stays as it is. On the frozen copy, 178 of the 255 issues with rows were closed on the merger's row alone, by the minute their rows share with their merge line. The split itself is two sub-headings inside the Evidence section, `### Proof` and `### Notes`, written when an empty section gets its first entry. Rows and 321's attachments go to Proof, after the last row, so row numbers never move. Notes, rulings, reasons and retraction entries go at the end, under Notes. A section that already holds content is never converted, and it reads, gates and prints as before. The frozen earlier reader reads the split form right: it refuses done on the merger's check alone, accepts it on a worker row and prints the deferral's reason. The claim that it would count the merger's row as proof is refuted, so the format is not raised, and acceptance line 5 is proposed in that wording for the reviewer to approve. Two top-level headings were measured as a silent misreading by the frozen reader (a false refusal, a lost reason, a third section on write), and that design would also break Horde's body edits. The journal gains no line shape, `RECORD_API` and every export stay as they are, and Horde needs no change. The plan is five commits, each checked by the suite and by the golden outputs of the frozen loop.
