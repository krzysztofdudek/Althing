# Critique: fidelity to the owner's intent

Three Critical, six Important, five Minor.

## Critical

**C1. The rule "reach and reversibility" fails its own test cases (README section 3; decision-rights.md, "The rule that settles most cases").**
The note defines a two-way door as something the loop can undo before anything leaves the machine, then calls the five stop cases of SKILL.md "five one-way doors". Four of them can be undone before shipping: a goal change, a weakened test, a number with no origin, and a lost conflict side, since git holds both sides.

The rule also drops two of the three reasons in method/working-model.md: consent the tools' law reserves, and what the family is or when a release ships. Read literally, a `yg-suppress`, a `review_by` change or an architecture edit is reversible and of the owner's reach, so "the jarl makes it provisionally". `quality-first-mandate` and AGENTS.md section 6 forbid that, and the table has no row for them.

Fix: put the owner's reserved list first, as a test of its own. Call the stop cases what they are: held by ruling.

**C2. The concept is a list of patches and never answers "primitive" (sections 3 to 5).**
The word "primitive" and the phrase "any scale" appear nowhere in the README. Section 3 opens with "the roles stay". Section 5.8 says the five exclusions "still hold, every one of them", although they were drawn for a composer that left on 2026-11-04. Section 4 is refusals, one view, brief additions and reports.

The scale stops at about seven coordinators of about seven units, then "another loop". Nothing says how two loops share an order, a view or the owner's digest. Nothing covers a change that must land in two repositories in sequence, and item 2 removes the one queue that gave an order.

Fix: either defend plainly that the model is right and name what was primitive, or add the layer above one loop and cross-repository landing, each with a label and a loss. Test each exclusion against his new word.

**C3. The marks and the labels do not meet, and speed is never mentioned.**
source.md says he wants 6.3.0 world class. Section 1 raises the bar, then the items that reach it wait:
- proof a reader can re-run, and testimony kept apart (marks 3 and 6, item 5);
- cost per issue, which mark 7 names in the wording he agreed to (item 9);
- stop-starting (mark 9);
- close-driven change (mark 10).

Mark 2's drill has no item and no row in the measurement table. No table says which marks 6.3.0 reaches.

Item 9's reason, "hosts report usage differently", is something section 7 admits was not checked. Items 5, 7, 8 and 10 give no reason for their label, and `no-effort-estimates` allows only quality, risk or truth.

The other flank is `release-fast-light-models-grouped-reviews`, which the README never cites. Six items labelled 6.3.0 enter a loop with 163 issues done and 32 open or in progress. Item 2 adds a line shape, so that ruling keeps it on the strong tier and out of grouped review.

Fix: one table of marks against 6.3.0 after its items, a permitted reason on every label, and the trade between world class and quick put to him.

## Important

**I1. `code-docs-conflicts` is put to him half quoted (section 3).** The concept flags its reading as his to confirm, then omits the ledger entry's second paragraph: the coordinator settles when one option clearly fixes and breaks nothing for adopters, and "asks when the choice breaks existing graphs or behaviour, or when it is not sure". **I4. A stop scoped by the raiser's reach undoes mark 1.** "The jarl's the loop" lets one stop halt everything while he sleeps, the failure decision-rights.md complains of one level down. Fix: a stop holds what depends on it.

**I5. The reversal of "no permission table" answers one of its two grounds (sections 4 and 5).** Section 5 answers "theatre" (F-I2). The same cut rested on M-I5, "policing, not recording". "The failure that happens" rests on one agent typing every role on a scratch loop; the README cites no case from the 6.2.0 or 6.3.0 records. The narrowing the 6.3.0 decisions do record was the jarl's. He looked at it by ratify (a-017), and on 2026-11-08 rejected a jarl's known limitation (a-018). Item 1 exempts that level, and the table gives post-lease Acceptance to the coordinator with no ratify.

Fix: answer M-I5, cite a real case or say none was found, and put post-lease narrowing in his digest.

**I6. The human organisation is answered in name only.** It gets one table row and one paragraph, marked built, never run, and absent from section 7. The table assumes one owner. **I7. The one table has no home (decision-rights.md).** The note counts nine scattered places and about 19,000 words for a coordinator. No item retires any of them, so the table becomes the tenth, against "one home per fact". Fix: an item that names the home and removes the rest.

**I8. "What exactly we lose" is thin where it matters ("Not to do").** Five of nine cells say "nothing" or an equivalent. Two say "nothing at this scale", in a concept for any scale. "Nothing Jarl should own" names no loss. Planted defects are dropped as "costly", a reason `no-effort-estimates` excludes. "Yggdrasil's instructions already reach every agent" is the assumption his rules-with-a-scope input disputes. Section 5's nine disagreements carry no label or loss.

**I9.
