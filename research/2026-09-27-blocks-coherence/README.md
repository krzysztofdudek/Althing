# Blocks and the composer: a coherence critique

**Question:** Is the "three blocks and one composer" concept, as ruled on 2026-09-27, coherent?

**Method:** An independent, read-only critic's reading of the concept against the thesis, the canon, the mapping report, the earlier Jarl, Horde and Yggdrasil research, the rulings and issues of the 6.1.0 loop, and the tools' READMEs and skills.

**Status:** concluded

**Conclusion:** The direction holds and the cut does not yet: two of three seams are in the wrong place, one block already exists inside Horde (the landing gate), and a 7.0-sized rebuild was put into a release paused for readiness; the owner stopped the rebuild the same day.

## What is here

- [report.md](report.md): the critique (the English original)
- [coordinator-notes.md](coordinator-notes.md): the coordinator's own notes to the owner, which agree and disagree with the critique and propose an order for 7.0
