# Blocks and the composer: Horde on Jarl's engine, Grain in the architect's hands

**Question:** If Yggdrasil, Grain and Jarl are independent blocks and Horde the composer on Jarl's loop engine, what moves where, and what does it cost?

**Method:** A read-only mapping of every Horde script against Jarl, Grain and Yggdrasil on their `release/6.1.0` branches, with line counts and citations.

**Status:** concluded

**Conclusion:** The direction is sound, but Horde's loop scripts duplicate Jarl only in the record, not in behaviour; Jarl's own boundary forbids most of what Horde's loop does, and only about 4,000–5,500 of Horde's ~23,100 script lines truly move to Jarl.
