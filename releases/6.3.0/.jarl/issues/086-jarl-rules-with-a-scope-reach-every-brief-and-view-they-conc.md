## What
The owner asked for rules that attach not only to the loop or one issue but to a role, a tag and other scopes, delivered without anyone having to remember them (README of research/2026-11-03-jarl-as-a-loop-tool). Report section 6. Folds 020 (the loop-scoped rule), 061 and 062 (rulings in resume), 051 (the reviewer's rank) and the rule half of 050.

## Why
A rule written once in prose is lost when a brief is written by hand; in the field a rule the reviewer reference already held never reached the reviewers (058's evidence).

## Acceptance
- decide takes an Applies to with one or more selectors joined by and (the loop, a role, a tag, an issue and its sub-issues, a kind, a slice, an area of files, a repository, a moment of work) and an end (revoked, a date, an issue closing)
- brief and resume --as print the rules that match, whole, and nothing else; resume no longer prints every ruling in force
- An agent can propose a rule with the ball on the jarl, or on the owner where the rule is his; once accepted it reaches every matching brief
- Jarl ships built-in rules quoted from the role references, at least: reviewer, every round: re-check every acceptance line; reviewer, light-tier work: re-run each command a proof row names; reviewer, format, contract or visual work: a tier above the worker; coordinator: the four reasons to escalate; coordinator: pull before leasing, push right after; one shared review only over issues that are one contract or one screen, at most four
- close offers each loop rule for promotion: carried into the next loop, written into the repository's agent instructions, written into Yggdrasil's law when it is about code (as area rulings are today), or dropped
- Whether Applies to raises the record format is checked and stated; tests for matching, ending, proposing and promotion
