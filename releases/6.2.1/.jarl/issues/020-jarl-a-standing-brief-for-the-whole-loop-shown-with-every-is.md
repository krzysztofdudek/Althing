## What
A coordinator needed every worker to carry the same working rules (where to write, which rulings bind, how to treat defects) and attached them as notes under Evidence on fifty-odd issues; amendments made while workers ran went by message and are not in the record.

## Why
Rules every worker must follow belong to the loop, once, in the record, not copied into fifty issues or lost in messages.

## Acceptance
- The loop can hold a standing brief, set and amended by a command that writes a log line
- show and the worker's view print it with each issue, with the date of its last amendment
- Tests
