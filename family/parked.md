# Parked ideas

Ideas the owner has judged real but not for now. Each entry gives the idea in one paragraph, why it is parked, and what would un-park it. Nothing here is work: work exists only as an issue in a release loop. The date in each heading is when the idea was parked.

## Law commons and an enterprise layer above the family (parked 2026-09-09)

**The idea.** "Say it once" is per repository today: every repository starts from zero law except what Grain mines from its own history. A shared, evidence-carrying library of rules (each with its drill corpus and its provenance) would let a rule written once be adopted anywhere its evidence fits, with Grain proposing which library rules a repository's code already keeps. One level up, an enterprise layer could give architects and developers tools to steer rules globally across a company's repositories: which aspects are mandatory where, which are advisory, who may lower them, with the same raise-freely, lower-with-signature procedure the family uses inside one repository.

**Why parked.** Krzysztof, 2026-09-09 (translated from Polish): "For now I have not assumed that rules could flow out into any kind of commons. That would need thinking through in depth; the topic is parked until who knows when." Rules flowing out of a repository raise questions the family has not thought through: what a rule carries when it leaves its evidence, who owns a shared rule, and how a company-wide rule meets a repository's own law and its client's veto.

**What would un-park it.** Adoption. Krzysztof, 2026-09-09 (translated from Polish): "If it turned out that the family catches on and there is adoption, an enterprise version could provide that kind of overarching layer." The seam already exists in shape: aspects are files with provenance, Grain proposes rules from evidence, and `yg adopt` accepts a proposal; a library would be one more source of proposals.

## One seat, several people (parked 2026-09-09)

**The idea.** The family has one human, the client. Organisations have several people with different keys: one orders, one answers for the law, one for the money. Whether the client's seat is a person or a role that several people can hold with different keys is undecided.

**Why parked.** The owner chose not to work on it yet. Nothing in 6.0.0 depended on the answer: every key the family had then was either the client's or the architect's, and both are functions a single person can hold.

**What would un-park it.** A client whose approvals come from more than one person, or the enterprise layer above.
